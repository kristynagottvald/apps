Introduction

Marketing attribution
can help

01

Marketing attribution is an overarching term used to describe how you can attribute each conversion — or sale — to a specific marketing touchpoint in any given campaign.

At its simplest, it can track the first or last marketing touchpoint in the customer journey and attribute 100% of the credit to that touchpoint. At its most complicated, it can track every single marketing touchpoint, like the four stops in the journey on the right.

Multi-touch attribution is the most complex of the models because it takes into account every step. A potential customer watches a YouTube video and skips your ad. Later they click a sponsored Google result, then ignore one of your emails, and finally buy after a Facebook ad.

Choosing a model

for you?

03
Tip 1

Match the model to your sales cycle

Short, single-channel journeys are well served by a single-touch model. The longer and more varied the path to purchase, the more a multi-touch model will show you that a last-touch report hides.

Start by mapping the touchpoints a typical customer meets before they buy, and count how many there are.

Tip 2

Compare models before you commit

Run two or three models over the same period and watch where the credit moves. Channels that gain credit under multi-touch models are the ones doing quiet work earlier in the journey.
