01 · Framework

The frame comes first.

Every Thread piece sits inside the same hairline frame: a rail on the left, a line under the top edge, a line above the bottom edge. Type and logos hang off those lines, and a yellow panel takes the rest. Switch the format and the frame follows.

Template

Format

Show

  • 131The rail. A full-height hairline, 131 units in from the left on a 2000 wide canvas.
  • 45 / 47The top and bottom lines. Posts end on them, so the frame always reads as closed.
  • 9.85%The text margin. Headlines, sub lines and logos start here, never on the rail.
  • 60.4%On 16:9 the yellow panel starts here. On taller formats it drops to the bottom.
02 · Voice & Tone

Say it like a text.

Thread is a conference about SMS, so we write the way a good text reads: short, warm, a little bit proud. Headlines run to three short lines. Anything quiet goes in italics. Dates are May 12–14 · New York.

Short.One idea per line. If the headline needs a second breath, break the line, don’t add a clause.
Warm.Talk to one person. Say “you”, say “we”, skip “attendees will be invited to”.
A bit proud.We’re allowed to be pleased. Put the confident bit in italics and let it sit.
CorporateThread
  • Sentence caseHeadlines are written the way you’d say them. Capitals are for the date and the tags.
  • Title CaseBadge names only: one line, like “Early Bird”. Short caps such as VIP stay as typed.
  • *Italics*Wrap a headline line in stars to set it in italics. One italic line, at most.
  • May 12–14 · New YorkDates and place on one line, set in tracked capitals.
03 · Logo

One swash, one wordmark.

The Thread logo is a calligraphic swash T joined to a serif wordmark. It lives bottom left, next to the Attentive logo, and always in the ink colour of its page. Resize it, and watch the minimum.

04 · Typography

Typography

Two families do the work. Instrument Serif carries the headlines, stretched 16% wide and tightened up. Inter carries everything small: sub lines, tags, dates. A script, Pinyon, is kept for the one big capital on a badge. Type your own below.

07 · Badges

A badge for every pass.

Five badge shapes say what kind of ticket, talk or session something is. Each one carries a big calligraphic capital with a yellow extrusion, a divider with two stars, and its name in Title Case on a single line. Pick one, rename it, save it.

Shape

Name · one line, Title Case

Ground

  • 1 OutlineA two-line keyline: a heavy outer edge, a thin one just inside. Sharp corners, always.
  • 2 CapitalThe first letter of the name in Pinyon Script, stepped back into a yellow extrusion with hairline contours.
  • 3 DividerA hairline at two-thirds height with a small star at each end.
  • 4 NameInstrument Serif, Title Case, one line. It shrinks to fit, it never wraps.
08 · Motion

Written on, then lifted off.

Motion is a pen. Calligraphy loops write themselves on, hold, and un-write from the tail, each stroke 0.55 seconds behind the last. Hairlines do the same. Everything repeats every 8 seconds, so a clip loops with no jump. Drag the playhead.
